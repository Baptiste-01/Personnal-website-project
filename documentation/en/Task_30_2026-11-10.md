- **Status:** Completed

## 🎯 Objective

1. **Fast Tag Banning:** Allow players and hosts to exclude specific tags in one click without selecting all other tags (e.g., banning `#Personnage` in `Anime & Manga`), persisted in Game Templates and synced via WebSockets.
2. **Smart Collapsible Dropdowns:**
   - Collapse categories with more than 10 tags (e.g., `Mythologie & Légende`) into a searchable dropdown by default.
   - Group `#Personnage` secondary series tags (`One Piece`, `Naruto`, `Bleach`, etc.) in `Anime & Manga` under a dedicated searchable/scrollable sub-menu.
   - Collapse the Admin `Tags disponibles` section by default (`showAvailableTags = false`) with search filtering and scroll.
3. **In-Game Difficulty Display (`FlashGuess`):** Show the active question's difficulty badge (`🟢 Easy`, `🟡 Normal`, `🔴 Hard`) above the prompt without leaking answers, hints, categories, or tags.

---

## 📋 Action Plan

1. Extend `GameSettings`, `CategoryInfo` (`tagChildren`), `Question`, and `Arena` with `bannedCategoryTags` and `bannedTags`.
2. Update `buildWhereClause` and `GET /questions/categories` in `apps/api/routes/questions.ts`.
3. Serialize category maps in `GameRoom.startGame`, expose `GameRoom.getPublicQuestion()` (including `difficulty`) across WS flows, and emit `room_error` if filtered results are empty.
4. Build the tag-ban UI, dropdowns (>10 tags, `#Personnage`, collapsible Admin tags), `Lobby` parameter forwarding, `TemplateModal`/`Profile` previews, and `FlashGuess` difficulty badge.
