## 🎯 Objective

Allow players and arena hosts to quickly **ban (exclude)** specific tags without manually selecting every other tag (e.g., banning `#Personnage` inside `Anime & Manga` in one click), while improving usability on large or nested tag lists:
1. **Fast Tag Banning**: `[ 🎯 Inclure | 🚫 Bannir ]` mode toggle + right-click quick-ban shortcut, persisted in Game Templates (Task 29) and synchronized over WebSockets.
2. **Collapsible Dropdown** when a category contains more than 10 tags (e.g., `Mythologie & Légende` with 13 tags) with quick text search filtering.
3. **`Anime & Manga` `#Personnage` Specific Sub-Tag Dropdown**: dynamically group secondary tags co-occurring exclusively with `Personnage` (`One Piece`, `Naruto`, `Bleach`, etc.) into a dedicated dropdown so players can include or ban individual series or the entire `#Personnage` group.
