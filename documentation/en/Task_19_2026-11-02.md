## Objective
1. **Remove all fake accounts from arenas and end-game podium:**
   - Eliminate hardcoded dummy accounts (`Alex_Pro`, `Marie42`) from the arena waiting room and the FlashGuess results podium.
   - Load and display only the authentic logged-in user profile (from `localStorage`'s `auth_user`) or default to "Vous".
2. **Functional player limit with an Unlimited option:**
   - Make the player limit setting interactive and fully functional.
   - Provide an explicit **∞ Unlimited** option (represented by `maxPlayers = 0`).
   - Allow fast selection using preset pills (`2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 16, 20`).
   - Provide a range slider (2 to 50 players) for precise adjustments.
   - Immediately persist changes in PostgreSQL through `PATCH /arenas/:id`.
   - Provide the same player limit controls within the Lobby's arena creation modal (`CreateArenaModal`).
   - Prevent joining full arenas from the Lobby and display a `Full` status badge.
