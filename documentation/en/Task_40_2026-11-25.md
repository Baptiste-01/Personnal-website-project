## 🎯 Objective
Resolve all UI, persistence, scoring, and filtering issues identified in FlashGuess / SpeedSmik multiplayer:
1. **FlashGuess Typography & Layout**: Fix illegible digit `5`, fit the full question area (timer, prompt, image, input/reveal) cleanly inside `100dvh` without vertical scrolling, and fix overlapping player card badges in the sidebar.
2. **Images & Final Round Reveal**: Preload upcoming images, prevent stale previous-round images from lingering while loading, and show the final question's answer reveal for 3.5s before displaying results.
3. **Exact Degressive Scoring**: Award exact `pointsPerAnswer` (e.g., 100 pts) to the first correct answerer and decay linearly down to 10% for subsequent players without hidden multipliers.
4. **Arena Lobby Persistence & UX**: Preserve modified arena settings across game ends, returns, and `F5` refreshes, pre-fill the default arena title with the username (`Arène de <username>`), and make the entire category bar clickable.
5. **Strict Per-Category Difficulty Filtering**: Fix alias key overwrites in `categoryDifficulties` (`drapeau` vs `drapeaux`, `capitale` vs `capitales`, etc.) so category-level difficulty always overrides lobby default difficulty.

---

## 🛠️ Implementation

### 1. FlashGuess Typography & Viewport Layout (`index.html`, `index.css`, `FlashGuess.css`, `FlashGuess/index.tsx`)
- Loaded a dedicated digit-only Google Fonts subset (`Outfit` with `&text=0123456789`) in `apps/web/index.html` and added `--font-digits: 'Outfit'` at the front of `--font-display`, `--font-pixel`, and `--font-body` in `index.css` so all digits (`0-9`) render crisply without altering `Pixelify Sans`, `Press Start 2P`, or `Inter` for letters and symbols, alongside `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on `.fg-metric-val`.
- Locked `.fg` to `100dvh` on desktop with a widened `1040px` flex-column `.fg-question-area` keeping `.fg-progress-bar` pinned at the top, `.fg-prompt` / `.fg-question-image` (`clamp(120px, 34vh, 360px)`) dynamically sized in the middle, and `.fg-answer-area` / `.fg-reveal` pinned at the bottom.
- Refactored `.fg-player-card` into a clean 2-row layout (`fg-player-row` + wrapping `fg-player-badges`) inside a `280px` `.fg-sidebar` to eliminate all badge overlaps and overflows.

### 2. Image Preloading, Final Reveal & Degressive Scoring (`roomManager.ts`, `useArenaSocket.ts`, `FlashGuess/index.tsx`)
- Added `key={`${currentQuestion.id}-${currentQuestion.imageUrl}`}` to `<img>` with `imageLoaded`/`imageError` state and background preloading of `nextImageUrl`.
- Deferred `this.finishGame()` by 3.5s via `this.revealTimeout` in `endRound()` when `isGameOver` is true.
- Updated `submitAnswer()` to award exact `basePoints` to the first player and linear decay toward `max(10, 10%)` based on elapsed delay after `firstAnswerTime`.

### 3. Settings Persistence, Default Title & Clickable Category Bar (`Arena/index.tsx`, `Lobby/index.tsx`)
- Added per-arena `localStorage` persistence (`smik_arena_settings_<arenaId>`) prioritized on mount, stripped creation query params via `window.history.replaceState` to survive `F5` refreshes, and added non-destructive server hydration via `hasHydratedFromServerRef` / `skipNextServerSyncRef`.
- Pre-filled default arena name with `Arène de ${getLocalPlayer().name}` in `CreateArenaModal`.
- Made the full `.category-card-header` bar clickable to toggle categories in both `Lobby` and `Arena`.

### 4. Strict Category Difficulty & Alias Deduplication (`questions.ts`, `roomManager.ts`, `tagFilter.ts`, `Arena`, `Lobby`)
- Exported `getCanonicalCategoryKey` and `getDifficultyQueryVariants` (`facile`/`easy`, `moyen`/`normal`/`medium`, `difficile`/`hard`) in `questions.ts`.
- Added canonical deduplication in `serializeCategoryMap` and `startGame()` prioritizing exact `settings.categories` keys over stale alias keys, backed by an in-memory difficulty filter safeguard.
- Synchronized alias keys client-side via `setCanonicalCategoryDifficulties` and kept global difficulty toggles consistent across all categories.
